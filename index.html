Privacy Policy for Cubino

Effective date: October 7, 2026
App: Cubino (package com.cubino.game)

This Privacy Policy explains how Cubino (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Cubino mobile game on Android (and related platforms where the App is distributed). We aim to be transparent about advertising, local storage, and third-party services.

1. Who we are

Cubino is a puzzle game. For privacy questions, contact us via GitHub Issues or through the developer contact details on the Cubino Google Play listing.

2. Information we store on your device

The App stores gameplay-related data locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress (journey levels, daily challenge status)
  • Coins and cosmetic unlocks from the in-game shop
  • Preferences such as language, sound, and music settings

This data stays on your device unless you clear the App’s storage or use the in-app “Delete all data” option in Settings. We do not operate a Cubino account system and we do not collect this progress on our own servers.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Cubino shows ads (for example rewarded videos and interstitials) using Google AdMob (and related Google advertising technologies). On some web builds, advertising may also use Google AdSense for Games.

These advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID / device advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, whether a rewarded ad was completed)
  • Diagnostic or fraud-prevention signals used to serve and measure ads

Google may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud or abuse, subject to your device settings and applicable consent choices (for example GDPR/UMP consent forms where required).

Learn more about Google’s practices: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & data use.

4. Permissions and device signals

The App requests network access to load game assets where needed and to request ads. Advertising SDKs may use the advertising identifier and related network/ad services permissions provided by the platform.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings on the device
  • To show ads that support the free game
  • To comply with law and protect against abuse or fraud (via advertising partners)

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising-related data is processed by Google and may be shared by Google with its advertising partners according to Google’s policies. We do not share your local game progress with third parties.

7. Children

Cubino is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or its advertising partners, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Your choices

  • Ads & personalization: Use your device’s ad personalization / advertising ID settings, and any in-ad or consent prompts shown by Google.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Danger zone → Delete all data, or clear Cubino’s app storage in system settings.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes the local App data from the device.

9. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising partners retain data according to their own retention policies.

10. International users

Advertising partners may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required by law, consent mechanisms (such as Google’s User Messaging Platform) are used before ads are requested.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: https://github.com/Harut1991/cubino/issues
